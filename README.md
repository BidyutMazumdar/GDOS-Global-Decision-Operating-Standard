# GDOS™ — Global Decision Operating Standard

## A Unified Global Framework for Decision Intelligence

GDOS™ (Global Decision Operating Standard) is a next-generation framework designed to standardize how decisions are made across governments, corporations, defense systems, and AI-driven infrastructures.

---

## The Global Problem

Modern systems have achieved:
- Advanced Data Infrastructure  
- Artificial Intelligence  
- Expert-driven Policy Systems  

However, the world still lacks a **unified decision standard**.

This results in:
- Policy inconsistency  
- Strategic misalignment  
- Unpredictable crisis responses  
- Fragmented global governance  

---

## The GDOS Solution

GDOS introduces a structured decision framework combining:

- Data Integrity  
- Context Awareness  
- Risk Projection  
- Ethical Weighting  
- Noise Reduction  

---

## Core Decision Equation

GDOS Score = (Data Integrity × Context Awareness × Risk Projection × Ethical Weight) / Noise
